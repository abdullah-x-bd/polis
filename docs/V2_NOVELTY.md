# POLIS v2 Novelty Target

POLIS v2 is not designed merely to show that an executable rule can block an unsafe action. That observation is already too weak for the intended research contribution.

The intended contribution is a controlled experimental framework for studying **institutional safety-performance frontiers** in multi-agent AI systems.

The paper-level novelty rests on five linked questions:

1. How does governance effectiveness change as optimization pressure conflicts with policy?
2. Can executable denial remain safe while allowing productive recovery rather than forcing failure?
3. How much of a provenance institution's advantage comes from information versus external enforcement?
4. Do institutions reduce sensitivity to heterogeneous model-team composition?
5. Can the representation of an institution itself create behavioral responses, such as numerical quota focal points?

The study therefore treats institutions as experimental mechanisms whose direct effects, behavioral responses, externalities and failure modes are measured rather than assumed.
