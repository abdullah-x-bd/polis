# POLIS v2 Inference Budget Plan

The scientific design is not chosen to exhaust a budget. The budget exists only to determine how broadly the frozen design can be executed.

## Full cheap-model backbone

Models:

- Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite
- Mistral Small 4
- GPT-4.1 Mini
- DeepSeek V4 Flash

These models run the complete main Delegation, wording robustness, heterogeneous-team and Commons studies.

## Frontier diagnostic

Qwen3.7 Max, Claude Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.5 are restricted to the pre-specified 24 high-conflict Delegation scenarios under four key governance regimes.

## Hard budget behavior

Every execution shard has its own explicit dollar ceiling and a pre-request reserve. Provider-reported cost is appended to a persistent ledger after each paid request. Shards are resumable, so retries do not intentionally repurchase completed episode keys.

The frontier diagnostic may be omitted only if the account balance is insufficient after the complete cheap-model backbone. Such omission is a budget constraint, not a result-dependent decision. The cheap-model confirmatory backbone therefore has priority over the frontier subset.
