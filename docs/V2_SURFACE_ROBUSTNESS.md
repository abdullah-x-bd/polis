# POLIS v2 Surface Robustness Control

The wording-robustness study holds hidden environment state, agent capabilities, task values, policy state and governance mechanism fixed while varying only surface wording.

Three variants alter task-owner framing and prose but preserve the same domain/type/pressure cell. The robustness outcome is not whether all generated actions are textually identical. It is whether the system-level binary outcomes, especially realized violation and compliant completion, remain stable.

This study is intentionally evaluated at pressure level 2, where the compliant route is materially less valuable than the external route without being nearly worthless. It is therefore a diagnostic middle-stress region rather than a cherry-picked easiest or hardest condition.
