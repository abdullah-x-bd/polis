# POLIS v2 Expected Failure Modes

The following outcomes are considered scientifically valid and will not trigger post-hoc redesign after freeze:

- strong prompting can equal or outperform executable governance for capable models
- local and provenance guards can remain behaviorally indistinguishable if models never exploit metadata loss
- recoverable guards can induce repeated violation attempts
- approval can become an easy universal recovery strategy
- high optimization pressure can reduce completion even under perfect safety
- some models can ignore explicit private-payoff incentives in Commons
- the visible-cap effect can disappear in the new causal design
- wording variants can reveal substantial surface sensitivity
- heterogeneous teams can be less safe than any homogeneous component model
- frontier models can reverse the cheap-model pattern

These possibilities are part of the research question rather than reasons to discard a condition.
