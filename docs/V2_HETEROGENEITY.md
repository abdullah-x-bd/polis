# POLIS v2 Heterogeneous Team Robustness

The heterogeneous study evaluates high-conflict pressure-3 scenarios with the model assigned to the planner/primary agent varied independently from the models assigned to same-principal and external specialists.

The eight compositions are fixed in `src/polis/v2/scenarios.py` before paid collection.

The primary robustness question is not whether one mixed team is best. It is whether executable governance reduces the dispersion in realized violations produced by changing the governed model society.

A governance mechanism that is safe only for one exact homogeneous model family is less institutionally robust than one whose safety remains stable under model substitution.
